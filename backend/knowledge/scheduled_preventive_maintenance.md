# Heavy Construction Equipment Preventive Maintenance Matrices
## Standard Service Intervals (Caterpillar 320, XCMG XE215, Sany SY215, Komatsu PC200)

### 1. Daily / 10 Operating Hours Inspection Checklist
- **Walkaround Visual Safety Inspection**:
  - Check engine oil dipstick (level between crosshatch marks).
  - Inspect coolant recovery tank level (do not open pressurized radiator cap when hot).
  - Inspect hydraulic oil tank sight glass with cylinders positioned in standard OEM measuring stance (arm in, bucket curled, rested on ground).
  - Drain water separator bowl on primary fuel filter to purge water and algae before startup.
  - Grease all high-wear linkage pins: bucket hinge pins, arm cylinder rod eye, boom pivot pins (use Lithium EP2 grease).
  - Check air filter dust evacuator valve (squeeze rubber duckbill to eject coarse dust).
  - Inspect track tension / sag: 25-40 mm sag between carrier roller and track shoe for steel crawler tracks.

---

### 2. 250 Operating Hours Service Interval
- **Engine Lubrication & Air Service**:
  - Drain engine oil while warm; replace engine oil filter with OEM full-flow element.
  - Inspect serpentine drive belts for rib cracking, fraying, and proper tensioner stroke.
  - Clean radiator, oil cooler, and air-to-air aftercooler exterior cooling fins using compressed air or water spray directed from engine side out.
  - Inspect battery terminal clamps for sulfate corrosion; clean and apply dielectric grease.
  - Take fluid sample for SOS (Scheduled Oil Sampling) spectroscopy analysis to track wear metals (Fe, Cu, Al, Cr, Pb).

---

### 3. 500 Operating Hours Service Interval
- **Fuel System & Planetary Final Drives**:
  - Replace primary fuel filter (water separator element) and secondary 2-micron high-pressure fuel filter.
  - Prime fuel circuit completely using hand primer pump before cranking.
  - Replace hydraulic return oil filter cartridge inside reservoir top housing.
  - Inspect and change planetary travel final drive gearbox gear oil (API GL-5 80W-90 or 85W-140).
  - Inspect swing reduction gearbox oil level and condition.
  - Check pilot filter element: Replace spin-on pilot filter canister.

---

### 4. 1000 Operating Hours Service Interval
- **Major Powertrain & Hydraulic Adjustments**:
  - Change swing reduction planetary drive gear oil.
  - Replace hydraulic reservoir air breather filter element.
  - Inspect engine valve lash (tappet clearance): Adjust intake and exhaust valve clearances with feeler gauges per engine spec (typically Intake: 0.30 mm, Exhaust: 0.50 mm cold).
  - Inspect turbocharger shaft play and compressor wheel for abrasive dust erosion.
  - Check engine damper rubber condition for radial displacement or vulcanized bond separation.

---

### 5. 2000 Operating Hours Service Interval
- **Full Hydraulic & Cooling System Overhaul**:
  - Drain entire hydraulic oil reservoir; flush system; refill with new ISO VG 46 anti-wear hydraulic oil.
  - Clean or replace suction strainer basket located inside hydraulic tank bottom port.
  - Drain and flush engine cooling system; replace with heavy-duty extended life coolant (OAT 50/50 premix).
  - Inspect pilot control accumulator nitrogen pre-charge pressure (replace or recharge if under 1.5 MPa).
  - Perform hydraulic pump high-pressure relief valve pressure calibration across all main working circuits.
